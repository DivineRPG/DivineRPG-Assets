## WARNING: This document follows a running joke and should not be taken seriously.

### This is a list of all the features DivineRPG implemented **FIRST** and where Mojang definitely just copied our homework. It is sorted from definitely the same feature, to absurdly far-fetched. Lemme know if I missed any.

- **Netherite**
-- after 1.16 "The Nether update", the original Netherite had to be renamed to Torridite to minimize confusion. Also Red Nether Bricks are just Torridite Bricks.

- **Soul Fire**
-- blue fire and soul energy are old concepts in DivineRPG.

- **Custom Spawnegg Textures**
-- DivineRPG introduced mob-specific spawnegg textures in the 1.16.5 port.

- **Stained Glass & Colored Bundles**
-- having different color variants of blocks and items is nothing new to DivineRPG. For now, Mojang have only copied these colorful ideas for stained glass and colored bundles from colored armor pouches.

- **Pale Garden & The Creeking**
-- Skythern & Soul Fiend, but in the Overworld.

-- DivineRPG Crates worked the same way. To get rid of duplicate functionality, their behavior got changed after the Pots were updated in Minecraft.
